What Is HRL Country

A Community Built Like a Country

Citizenship, rights, an elected Council and shared infrastructure, run as the developer community of HRL International.

Citizenship

Join, Get an ID, Have a Voice

Every member becomes a Citizen with a unique ID (HRL-0001, HRL-0002, …) and a digital ID card. Residents and Founders vote, and new Visitors serve a 30-day probation.

Self-Governance

Assembly, Council & Steward

All Citizens form the Assembly. A five-member Council is elected every six months. The Steward appointed by the Company keeps everything legal, secure and funded.

Privacy First

Self-Hosted & Pseudonymous

Git hosting and end-to-end encrypted chat run on our own servers and are also reachable as Tor onion services. Pseudonyms are welcome, and we collect only the minimum data we need.

Recognition

The Contribution Ledger

Code, docs, reviews, mentoring and infrastructure work earn contribution points on a public leaderboard. Points are for recognition only: not currency, and not equity.

Founding Document

The Charter of HRL Country

Ten articles that define how we join, decide and resolve disagreements. Summary below.

Art. I · Nature & Legal Status

HRL Country is a private, membership-based developer community operated by HRL International Private Limited.

  • It is not a state and claims no territory or exemption from law.
  • The laws of India apply, and if this Charter conflicts with them, the law prevails.

Art. II · Citizenship

Open to anyone aged 18 or over who accepts the Charter and Code of Conduct.

  • Admission: approval by one Council member, or vouching by two Citizens.
  • Classes: Resident, Visitor (30-day probation, no vote) and Founder.
  • Leave any time, and your data is deleted on request.

Art. III · Rights of Citizens

Every Citizen is guaranteed:

  • Privacy and pseudonymity.
  • Free expression within the Code of Conduct.
  • Ownership of their own code, with OSI licences for community projects.
  • The right to propose, vote and stand for the Council, and to due process before any sanction.

Art. IV · Duties of Citizens

Membership comes with responsibilities:

  • Follow the Code of Conduct.
  • No illegal use of the infrastructure: no malware, fraud, harassment or piracy.
  • Never dox another Citizen. Report security issues responsibly.

Art. VI · Proposals & Voting

Transparent, predictable decision-making:

  • A motion needs 3 seconds, then 7 days of discussion and 5 days of voting.
  • Quorum: 20% of eligible voters.
  • Ordinary motions need a simple majority. Charter amendments and Council removals need a two-thirds majority.

Art. VII · Dispute Resolution

A fair, staged process:

  • Mediation first, then a Moderation Panel of 3 randomly chosen Citizens, then one appeal to the Council.
  • Sanctions: warning, suspension of up to 90 days, or revocation.
  • Nothing restricts anyone's right to go to the courts or the authorities.
