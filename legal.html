🔒 Global Privacy Policy (GDPR / CCPA & Zero-Cloud Guarantee)

HRL International Private Limited operates on a fundamental principle of Absolute Local Data Sovereignty. We build on-device GPU architectures that do not harvest, inspect, or upload creative user data.

  • Local GPU Shaders: All frame transformations, 12-bit DPX sensor calculations, neural depth estimations (ONNX), and ACEScc color science execute entirely on the user's local hardware (Apple Silicon / NVIDIA RTX).
