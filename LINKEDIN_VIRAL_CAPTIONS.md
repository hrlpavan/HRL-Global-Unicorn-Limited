## Strategic Posting Guidelines for Maximum Algorithmic Reach
1. **The 3-Line Hook Rule**: The first 2–3 lines dictate 90% of post impressions. Always place the core counter-intuitive claim or arresting metric *above* the "see more" fold.
2. **Spacing & Readability**: Single-sentence paragraphs, bullet points, and high white space optimize for mobile dwell time (LinkedIn's primary ranking signal).
3. **Zero External Link Penalty**: Never place raw links in the main post body. Add links in the **first comment** ("🔗 Links and live architecture docs below") or direct users to the bio/featured section.
4. **Visual Pairing**: Pair each post with a high-resolution screenshot from the official website, terminal output, or architectural diagram (never stock photos).
