## How to Add Your Experience to LinkedIn (60-Second Guide)

1. Open your LinkedIn profile and navigate to your **Experience** section.
2. Click the **+** (Add) button and select **Add position**.
3. Copy and paste each field below from your designated section.
4. Paste the **Description** into the description field (bullet formatting and line breaks are preserved).
5. Add the recommended **Top Skills** into LinkedIn's skills picker.
6. Under **Media**, click **Add link** and paste:  
   `https://hrlpavan.github.io/hrl-international-website-/leadership.html`
7. Click **Save** and check **Notify network** (optional, recommended for corporate launch visibility).
