HRL International Corporate Flag

The official sovereign flag and heraldic vector identity system engineered for HRL International Private Limited.
Built upon an 83px modular unit Nordic Cross geometry, quad-stripe canton accents, the official HRL brand insignia, dual heraldic eagles, and the 8-pointed compass star. Available in 100% scalable vector graphics and RAW Master Ultra-HD raster resolution.

📐 83px Modular Grid System

Precision 1024 × 682 layout based on an 83-pixel modular unit. Vertical white cross bar (x: 85..168) and horizontal bar (y: 297..380) define a golden 3:2 proportion.

🦅 Dual Heraldic Eagles

Bilateral double-headed eagle emblems in pure black (#000000) flanking the lower hoist canton (x: 6..77 and x: 177..247) symbolizing sovereign strength, vigilance, and authority.

⭐ 8-Pointed Compass Star

Crimson red (#BF1F1F) nautical star centered on the vertical cross stripe at (125.5, 428) with 4 cardinal points (r=44px) and 4 diagonal points (r=22.5px).

🛡️ Official HRL Brand Insignia

Stylized crimson red 'H' (x: 32..84) and pure black serif 'R' and flowing calligraphic 'L' (x: 104..219) seamlessly integrated across the horizontal white cross bar.

🎨 Replaceable Background Field

The background field is mapped to CSS custom variables (--field-color and --bg-color) and the #flag-background element for instantaneous color switching or transparent rendering.

⚡ 100% Vector Scalability

Native SVG with zero raster dependencies, crisp rendering across micro-favicons, GitHub README cards, company website headers, 4K monitors, and large-format physical textile prints.

🖼️ RAW Master UHD PNG (10,800 × 7,200 px)

77.7 Megapixels master raster asset (1.8MB RGBA) engineered for physical flag silk manufacturing, headquarters exterior banners, and ultra-high-definition corporate branding.
